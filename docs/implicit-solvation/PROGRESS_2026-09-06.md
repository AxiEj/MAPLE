# Route 1 progress snapshot — 2026-09-06

This is a **work-in-progress publication**, not full scientific or task admission.

## Included progress

- Prepared-topology CHA-GB provider caching and its existing parity evidence;
  retrospective ranking, charge-continuity, component and surface diagnostics;
  water/methanol/ethanol 3D-RISM numerical pilots and their explicit limitations.
- The new exploratory policy removes mandatory experimental-error thresholds.
  The reproducible source-panel selector requests 20 distinct solutes per solvent;
  the current 15-solvent MNSol panel supplies 222 of the requested 300 pairs.
  This is source selection, not completed multi-solvent energy/scoring work.
- Continuous sharp sphere-union volume and sigma-decomposed dispersion kernels
  replace voxel/dot counting in an **isolated nonpolar numerical prototype**.
  They introduce no smoothing width, fitted residual, or new solvent parameters.
  The composition probe keeps cavity radii `rmin + 1.3 A` distinct from dispersion
  surface radii `rmin + 0.557 A`, applies the audited mixing/density constants,
  reports coordinate gradients and their negative forces, and remains outside
  the runtime CHA provider. Dispersion quadrature's error estimate covers z only;
  azimuth-order convergence requires a separate paired-order check.
- Exact AmberTools 26 RC7 output-only precision builds were tested locally.
  The ordinary MAPLE parser already preserves the additional digits. The small
  [patch/build recipe record](benchmarks/route1_precision_output_patches_v1.json)
  is published; upstream sources, binaries and local build trees are not.
- Pre-publication review fixes reject boolean geometry before numeric coercion
  and prevent mutation of one result's capability flags from affecting another.

## Unfinished / not claimed

The CHA polar derivative remains unstable under the tested grid/step refinement.
Full CHA forces, OPT, FREQ and TS are **not enabled**. The nonpolar prototype's
correctness and timings do not certify the complete solvent potential, routine
performance, new hydration accuracy, or non-water runtime support.

Existing 3D-RISM consistency failures are retained as negative evidence. No
force substitution, experimental fitting or threshold-driven result selection
was introduced to bypass them.

## Evidence and publication boundary

The last local methanol nonpolar probe reported a selected-coordinate gradient
versus finite-difference discrepancy of approximately `2.95e-10 kcal/mol/A`.
It is a limited historical smoke, not an all-record/full-CHA force result.
Its source/script hashes precede the boolean/capability-isolation fixes above;
those old local artifacts have **not** been resealed or recertified. Fresh
software regression results must be distinguished from that historical run.

`.omx/` remains local: this includes license-controlled MNSol row data, input
parameters, raw numerical probes, downloaded Amber sources and compiled binaries.
Generated terminal scratch `mdout` is also excluded. Repository tests, source,
protocols, documentation and the distributable generated RISM assets are included.
