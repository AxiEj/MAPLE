# AIMNet2 point-charge smooth-harmonic conductor diagnostic

## Status and exact claim

This is a disabled, electrostatics-only research branch for the weak
geometry-mediated scalar.  It does not feed solvent state into AIMNet2 and
does not change the checkpoint, graph, inputs, hidden state, or charge output:

```text
c_A(R) = [q_AIMNet2(R), 0, 0, 0]
A(R) = E(R).T K(R) E(R)
S(R) = E(R).T V_point(R)
G_harm(R,c) = -1/2 (S(R)c).T A(R)^-1 (S(R)c)
F(R) = E_AIMNet2(R) + G_harm(R,c_A(R))
```

The parent continuum functional is

```text
Phi_harm(R,c,alpha)
  = 1/2 alpha.T A(R) alpha - alpha.T S(R)c,
d Phi_harm / d alpha = 0.
```

It is therefore a scalar-first geometry-dependent charge/conductor composite,
not fixed-geometry electronic mutual polarization.  AIMNet2's energy and
charge heads need not be conjugate for this weak composition because
`c_A(R)` is an explicit map rather than an independently optimized electronic
coordinate.  The AIMNet2 NQE charge construction remains the published model
identity ([AIMNet2, Chemical Science 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4SC08572H)).

This branch is deliberately named a **conductor reference**.  It has no
finite-dielectric solvent scaling, no solvent-specific physical configuration,
no CDS/nonpolar term, and no standard-state term.  The water radii used by the
optional real canary are a fixed diagnostic geometry configuration, not a
claim that this scalar is an admitted water solvation model.

## Reused architecture

The implementation does not introduce a second PCM stack.  It reuses the
existing fixed-dimensional harmonic components:

- `harmonic_exposure.py`: smooth invariant exposure coefficients and the
  rectangular product embedding `E(R)`;
- `harmonic_single_layer.py`: physical Coulomb single-layer matrix `K(R)`;
- `harmonic_torch_functional.py`: common rank/SPD gates, stationary solve,
  configuration binding, and same-scalar derivative path;
- `functional.py`: final `drive`, source JVP/VJP, mixed coordinate/source VJP,
  and fixed-source coordinate partial generated from one Torch scalar.

Only the source boundary map is new.  `harmonic_point_source.py` contains an
independent NumPy reference and a rigid-motion-invariant source/shell stratum
identity.  `harmonic_torch_primitives.py` contains the differentiable version.
The public provider is a thin specialization in
`harmonic_point_torch_functional.py`.

This separation is required: treating a point charge as an arbitrary
zero-width or first-channel Gaussian would change the physical source while
pretending to reuse the MACE-POLAR radial-GTO representation.

## Exact point-monopole map

Let target sphere `i` have radius `a_i`, and let
`d_ij = R_j - R_i`.  In the orthonormal real-harmonic convention, the Coulomb
potential of a unit point charge has boundary coefficients

```text
V_{i,lm;j}(R)
  = k_e 4*pi/(2*l+1)
    * r_<^l / r_>^(l+1)
    * Y_lm(d_ij / |d_ij|),

r_< = min(a_i, |d_ij|),
r_> = max(a_i, |d_ij|).
```

For `i=j`, only the `l=0` coefficient remains and equals
`k_e sqrt(4*pi)/a_i`.  The formula is the Laplace addition theorem used in
real-spherical-harmonic point-multipole continuum formulations.  Domain-
decomposition COSMO literature likewise expands both local surface unknowns
and point-multipole sources in real spherical harmonics
([Lipparini et al.](https://doi.org/10.1021/ct500998q)); the earlier ddCOSMO
work establishes the complete per-sphere harmonic discretization
([Cancès, Maday, Stamm](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4901304)).  MAPLE's weighted
Galerkin conductor is not relabelled as ddCOSMO: the citations justify the
harmonic/source algebra, not equation identity.

The source map has no laboratory angular grid.  Under a proper rotation `Q`,

```text
V_point(QR) = D_harm(Q) V_point(R)
```

on the point-monopole columns.  Combined with the existing `E` and `K`
intertwiners, this gives structural scalar invariance and coordinate-gradient
covariance up to floating-point/invariant-quadrature error.

## Reciprocity and derivatives

For fixed `R`,

```text
P_R = -S(R).T A(R)^-1 S(R)
```

is symmetric in the registered atomic `l<=1` pairing.  Only the monopole
subspace is active; the geometry-mediated scalar independently enforces exact
zero `l=1` AIMNet2 source components.  The reaction field is therefore the
full source-energy cotangent, not one half of it.

The total first derivative remains

```text
dF/dR = dE_AIMNet2/dR
      + partial_R G_harm(R,c)|c
      + (D_R c_A).T grad_c G_harm.
```

The continuum provider exposes `evaluate_field`, source JVP/VJP, and
coordinate VJP only as aliases of derivatives generated by the sealed scalar.
It also reports the actually measured dense stationarity residual and surface
condition number.  No independently coded receiver or force formula exists.

## Topology and regularity boundary

The retained harmonic coefficient dimension never changes, and smooth
exposure uses no active-node deletion.  This does not imply global `C2`:

1. a distinct point source crossing a target shell (`|d_ij| = a_i`) changes
   the inside/outside radial branch and is a first-derivative event;
2. the exact shell/shell Coulomb block is `C1` but generally not `C2` at
   internal or external sphere tangency;
3. AIMNet2 retains its own hard neighbor-list event surfaces;
4. a fully buried weighted chart can lose rank and fails closed.

The provider hashes every point-centre/target-shell inside/outside relation and
reports the minimum shell-event margin.  It never deletes coefficients, clips
eigenvalues, or regularizes a failed solve.  Current admissible language is
therefore only local to an event-free, full-rank stratum.

## Executed evidence and remaining blockers

Synthetic tests currently establish:

- addition-theorem coefficients versus an independent high-order direct
  surface projection for both inside and outside sources;
- exact-zero non-monopole columns;
- NumPy/Torch source-operator parity;
- source-direction finite differences, apply/adjoint reciprocity, mixed
  coordinate/source adjointness, and full coordinate finite differences;
- translation, proper-rotation, same-element permutation, net-force, and
  torque identities;
- a complete synthetic AIMNet2-charge chain rule through the registered
  geometry-mediated scalar;
- fail-closed point-on-shell events and measured stationary residuals.

The opt-in SHA256-bound real AIMNet2 water canary shows a materially narrower
result than the pyddx branch:

- the harmonic continuum alone is rotation invariant/covariant at roughly
  double-precision error;
- the full AIMNet2 plus harmonic scalar keeps the same model and continuum
  strata and passes the frozen rigid-rotation gate;
- the three-step full-energy directional finite-difference gate still fails
  because the real AIMNet2 energy head is float32 and the sampled differences
  do not form the required convergence window.

All public `E/F/H/V/M`, OPT, FREQ/TS/IRC, and MD flags remain false.  Before
force admission, the full real-stack directional, distorted-geometry,
closed-loop, cutoff/source-shell/tangency, and broader chemistry panels must
pass.  Hessian tasks additionally require the complete AIMNet2 contracted
charge Hessian and continuum `RR/Rq/qR/qq` HVP blocks on a full event-free `C2`
neighborhood.  Finite-dielectric physics and a separately derived same-scalar
nonpolar term are later, independent model-definition tasks; neither may be
inferred from this conductor diagnostic.
