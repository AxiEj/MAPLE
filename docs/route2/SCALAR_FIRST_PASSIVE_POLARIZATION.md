# Scalar-first passive-polarization head contract

## Status and scientific boundary

This is an **untrained architecture contract**, not an admitted Route-2 model.
It exists because the tested pre-existing candidates have terminal outcomes:

- original MACE-POLAR energy plus original source is not a common functional;
- its operational four-channel source and the one authorized fixed-radial
  repair fail matched quantitative PCM source gates;
- the changed MACE energy-gradient candidate is not passive or locally
  invertible at its tested state;
- unchanged MACE-MDP permanent charges/dipoles pass only `3/4` cases under a
  preregistered all-case gate, so no independent-polarization KKT construction
  is authorized on that identity.

No existing checkpoint is relabelled, corrected, or silently combined here.
`E/F/H/V/M` remain false.

## One scalar, no independent response implementation

For gauge-reduced dimensionless field coordinates `xi`, a trained head may
emit only:

```text
E0(R)       vacuum scalar
m0(R)       permanent reduced-source covector
C(R)        response factor
```

The framework, not the model adapter, owns the field scalar:

\[
E(R,\xi)
=
E_0(R)+m_0(R)^T\xi-
\frac12\lVert C(R)\xi\rVert^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\nabla_\xi E=m_0-C^TC\xi,
\qquad
-\nabla_\xi^2E=C^TC\succeq0.
\]

The source, JVP, VJP, field HVP, fixed-field coordinate gradient, and mixed
coordinate/field VJP are all generated by the sealed
`FieldEnergyFunctional` autograd graph. A model-specific head cannot override
those derivatives or the quadratic scalar.

The fixed-total-charge affine chart and constant-potential gauge are inherited
from one content-addressed `GaugeReducedDualityMap`. The new contract requires
the normalized public positive energy-dual sign; a model-native sign must be
absorbed before constructing the head.

## Provider boundary

The implementation is
`maple.solvation.models.passive_polarization.PassiveQuadraticFieldEnergy`.
It is model-family agnostic. A MACE, AIMNet, UMA, or another field-aware
implementation supplies the same narrow `TrainedPassivePolarizationHead`
contract:

```python
configuration_sha256()
parameter_state_sha256()
coefficients_torch(geometry, *, reduced_dimension)
```

The head must also bind:

- a head-specific `provider_id`, `model_profile_id`, and coupling ID;
- complete `ModelProvenance`;
- exact checkpoint and live parameter-state hashes;
- field convention and duality-map hash;
- dtype, device, chemical domain, and coordinate-frame policy;
- affirmative optimizer parameter-group audit with evidence SHA-256.

Construction additionally requires a typed `PassiveTrainingRunBinding` from
`maple.solvation.models.passive_training`.  The run binding must match the
head's exact checkpoint, live parameter state, inference code, optimizer
protocol/state, optimizer-group audit, runtime manifest, and retained training
log, plus a dataset-access audit showing that the blind partition was not read
during fitting or selection. Both the preregistration and run SHA-256 enter
the wrapper identity;
drift in either fails closed.

The wrapper rechecks configuration, parameter, implementation, provenance,
and duality identities. Drift fails closed. It imports Torch lazily and enters
the existing generic `ScalarFirstElectronicResponseAdapter`; the continuum
solver never branches on the model family.

## Training preregistration boundary

Before training, `PassiveTrainingPreregistration` freezes:

- exactly one train, validation, and blind dataset index;
- record SHA-256s and molecule-family SHA-256s for each split;
- disjointness of both records and molecule families, preventing conformer
  leakage across splits;
- the chemical, charge/spin, atom-count, minimum-distance, field-norm, dtype,
  and coordinate-frame domain;
- each QM/component target, unit, reference protocol, objective weight, and
  whether it is used for training, model selection, or independent admission;
- training/inference code, optimizer protocol, duality map, source/response/
  root gate protocols, and allowed random seeds.

The target whitelist excludes experimental or total solvation free energies.
Matched fixed-source PCM energy is admission-only and cannot be a fitting or
model-selection objective.  The blind split cannot be used for model
selection.  These restrictions prevent the scalar-first replacement from
becoming another post-hoc solvation fit or component-cancellation patch.

The preregistration and completed-run records are capability-neutral. Their
presence proves only a consistent training provenance chain; source physics,
root stability, forces, and `E/F/H/V/M` remain separate gates.

## What is structural

The code architecture guarantees, for every successfully evaluated point:

1. source and response derive from one scalar;
2. the reduced response Jacobian is self-adjoint;
3. `-H = C.T @ C` is positive semidefinite;
4. total charge and gauge use the registered affine duality chart;
5. coordinate derivatives differentiate the same graph;
6. an untrained or optimizer-unaudited head cannot be constructed;
7. no callable method turns on a capability.

`PassiveQuadraticPointCertificate` reports factor rank and singular values as
pointwise diagnostics. It deliberately cannot admit global passivity, coupled
root stability, or a public capability.

## What remains empirical and profile-specific

Factorization alone does **not** prove:

- the permanent source reproduces total-QM cavity MEP or fixed-source PCM
  energy;
- the response magnitude, dipole, polarizability, or finite-field curve is
  accurate;
- the combined electronic-continuum Hessian has a positive margin;
- the root is globally unique on a declared geometry/field domain;
- the representation is structurally `SO(3)` equivariant;
- forces, distorted PES, Hessians, frequencies, or MD are admitted.

Those claims require a real trained checkpoint and content-addressed evidence.
MACE-MDP polarizabilities may be used as a training/distillation target, but
the rejected unchanged MACE-MDP permanent q/p source cannot be silently reused
as the permanent term.

## Matched component and ledger decision contract

`maple.solvation.release.electrostatic_decomposition` now freezes the next
scientific decision without manufacturing reference data or enabling a tier.
For every fixed nuclear geometry, `MatchedElectrostaticReferenceCase` stores
only the three primitive, electrostatics-only QM quantities

\[
E_{\rm vac}[\gamma_0],\qquad
E_{\rm vac}[\gamma_\star],\qquad
E_{\rm PCM}^{\rm elec}[\gamma_\star],
\]

where the middle term is evaluated without reoptimizing
`gamma_star` in vacuum.  Distortion, continuum stabilization, and total
electrostatic solvation energy are derived rather than caller-supplied.  The
record rejects CDS/nonpolar terms, standard-state corrections, experimental
solvation labels, geometry changes, and unconverged vacuum/PCM densities.

`QuantitativeSourceGatePanel` binds the exact source identity to the same
case, geometry, cavity, topology, continuum, and directly projected QM
boundary RHS used by the matched references.  A failed case is retained as
negative evidence, but `assess_operational_electrostatic_ledgers()` refuses to
run unless **every** frozen source case passed.  This encodes the terminal
rule that a failed source cannot be rescued by selecting a favorable ledger.

Only after that gate may a `LedgerAssessmentPreregistration` compare candidate
distortion, continuum, and total components.  The case set, component budgets,
candidate priority, and all-case selection rule are frozen before blind
results.  Every candidate prediction additionally binds its exact formula,
configuration, state, evaluation artifact, and a passed energy-semantics
canary; a failed field-work/double-counting audit cannot enter the comparison.
Missing cases, mixed continuum identities, post-hoc priorities, FreeSolv/MNSol
totals, or component cancellation fail closed.  A selected ledger is merely
the one allowed to advance to force/PES gates; the report still admits no
`E/F/H/V/M` capability.

No current source reaches this decision point.  The original MACE-POLAR,
fixed-radial repair, and unchanged MACE-MDP source panels remain terminal
negative.  The new contract is therefore preparation for a future trained,
content-addressed scalar-first source, not evidence that such a source exists.

## Required successor sequence

A concrete successor receives a new profile and must proceed in this order:

1. preregister training data, source/response objectives, domain, and immutable
   thresholds;
2. train the permanent and response outputs together under the fixed scalar
   construction, with optimizer-group audit;
3. pass total charge, multipoles, far-field MEP, cavity-surface MEP, and matched
   fixed-source PCM energy gates;
4. pass energy/source directional derivatives, reciprocity, finite-field
   response, passivity, and coordinate derivatives;
5. establish a uniform contraction or strong-monotonicity/combined-Hessian
   certificate on a declared domain;
6. combine only with the harmonic-Galerkin continuum through the existing
   scalar-first adapters;
7. run same-profile force finite differences, distorted PES, closed-loop,
   symmetry, physical-component, accuracy, and public-admission gates.

Failure of the source gate terminates that head before continuum force work.
Failure of the coupled stability certificate restricts it to research or
terminates it; geometry-dependent eigenvalue clipping, response rescaling, or
post-hoc solvation fitting is not an allowed repair.
