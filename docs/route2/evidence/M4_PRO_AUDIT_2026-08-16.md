
For residual `r`, the loss is

```text
phi_delta(r) = delta^2 * (sqrt(1 + (r/delta)^2) - 1).
```

Its second derivative is strictly positive for every finite residual. For a
full-column-rank design `X`, the coefficient Hessian is
`X.T @ diag(phi''(r)) @ X`, hence positive definite. The objective is also
coercive because pseudo-Huber grows linearly in every nonzero design direction.
M4 therefore has exactly one coefficient vector in exact arithmetic without a
coordinate-space anchor.

loss convention below the declared `1.5 kcal/mol` project target. It is not a
